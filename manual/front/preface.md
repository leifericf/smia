# Preface

This manual is itself a Smia manuscript: every page you are reading was
produced by building the Markdown sources under `manual/` with Smia.
It is the platform dogfooding its own affordances, and it doubles as a
regression test — if a feature breaks, this book stops building.

*Smia* is Norwegian for *the forge* — the workshop where raw material is heated
and hammered into a finished thing. The name fits the tool's shape: you bring one
manuscript, and Smia forges it into many finished editions — screen and print
PDFs, a press-ready PDF/X, an EPUB, and a website — each hammered to the demands
of its medium. The tool is written in Clojure and runs on the JVM, which is where
the workshop keeps its anvil.

The manual is organized into parts. The first part covers authoring; the second
covers configuration, theming, and the build commands; the third covers the
structural and visual apparatus of a long-form book. An appendix catalogs the
error types, and the back matter holds a bibliography and an index — both
generated by Smia from the sources.

Front matter like this preface is numbered in roman numerals; the body switches
to arabic numerals at the first chapter.
